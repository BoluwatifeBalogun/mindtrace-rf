Questions

Before you upload.

Does MindTrace NG diagnose Alzheimer's disease?

No. It reports what a statistical model sees in one image. It is an academic prototype for study and decision support, and only a qualified clinician can make a diagnosis.

What kind of image should I upload?

A single grayscale axial slice of a brain MRI, saved as PNG, JPG or BMP, up to 8 MB. Colour photographs and blank images are rejected.

What was the model trained on?

A public set of {{ '{:,}'.format(M.dataset.total) }} labelled MRI slices, half non-demented and half with very mild, mild or moderate dementia. It was split {{ M.dataset.split.train }} / {{ M.dataset.split.validation }} / {{ M.dataset.split.test }} for training, validation and testing.

Who can see my records?

Only you. Records are tied to your account, passwords are stored as hashes, and other users see zone totals only.

Why a Random Forest and not a deep network?

It trains in minutes on a laptop, needs no GPU, and every input it uses is a named measurement that can be listed and ranked.
